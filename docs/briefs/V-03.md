```ts
export class Guide implements EventSource<GuideEvents> {
  constructor(options: {
    /** At least one point. She starts standing on the first. */
    path: readonly { x: number; z: number }[];
    speed?: number;        // 2.6 m/s
    /** She stops and waits when the follower is further than this behind her. */
    waitDistance?: number; // 5
    /** ...and sets off again once the follower is within this. */
    resumeDistance?: number; // 3
  });
  /** Horizontal position; y is not hers to know. */
  readonly position: { x: number; z: number };
  /** World yaw she faces: the direction of travel, or toward the follower while waiting. */
  readonly yaw: number;
  /** True while her feet are moving this step. */
  readonly moving: boolean;
  readonly leading: boolean;   // between lead() and arrival
  readonly arrived: boolean;
  /** Start walking the path. Does nothing if already leading or arrived. */
  lead(): void;
  /** Advance by dt. `follower` is the player's position. */
  step(dt: number, follower: { x: number; z: number }): void;
  on(...): Unsubscribe;
}
```
- Not leading: `step` does nothing, `moving` false.
- Leading: if she is waiting, she stays until the follower is within
  `resumeDistance`; if she is walking and the follower is further than
  `waitDistance`, she stops and waits. While waiting `yaw` turns to face
  the follower (`Math.atan2(dx, dz)`) and `moving` is false.
- Walking: move `speed * dt` along the path toward the next point; a step
  that passes a point carries the remainder on to the next segment. `yaw` =
  `Math.atan2(dx, dz)` of the segment being walked. `moving` true.
- Reaching the last point: `arrived` true, `leading` false, `moving` false,
  emit `arrived` once, and face the follower.
- A one-point path arrives on the first step after `lead()`.

## Tests
1. before `lead()` nothing moves.
2. on a straight 10 m path with the follower beside her she covers 2.6 m in 1 s of 60 steps (+-0.01) and faces along the path.
3. she turns the corner of an L-shaped path and the remainder carries over (total distance walked after n steps equals speed * time).
4. follower left behind (> 5 m): she stops, `moving` false, faces the follower; follower at 4 m: still waiting; follower within 3 m: walks again.
5. arrival emits `arrived` exactly once, position equals the last point exactly, further steps do nothing.
6. `lead()` twice does not restart.
7. one-point path arrives at once.
