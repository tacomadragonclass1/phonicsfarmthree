   (`TERRAIN_TILES`, `PROPS`, `PLAYER_START`, `BELL`, `PEDESTALS`, `BLOCKS`,

   `SliceWorld` shape as they are.
2. **Terrain tiles**: for each entry of `TERRAIN_TILES`, `build('terrain', { outline: false })`,

   `Layer.World`, blocksCamera true. Register ALL tiles before anything
   else is placed (ground heights depend on them).
3. **Footprint ground height**: buildings must not float on a slope. Add
   `function footprintY(x: number, z: number, radius: number): number` that
   samples `groundY` at the centre and at the four points (x±radius, z) and
   (x, z±radius) and returns the LOWEST. For a prop, `radius` = the largest
   of the x/z half extents of its manifest box collider, or the cylinder
   radius, or 0 for `kind: 'none'`.
4. **Props**: for each entry of `PROPS` (index `i`): build, place at
   `(x, footprintY, z)`, `rotation.y = yaw ?? 0`, add, register with id
   `` `${model}-${i}` ``, `Layer.World`, the manifest's `blocksCamera`.
   This replaces the old cottage and trees code.
5. **World edge**: four invisible box colliders (ids `edge-n/s/e/w`,
   `Layer.World`, blocksCamera false) just outside `WORLD_HALF_EXTENT`:
   each 1 m thick, 10 m tall, centred at y = 4, long enough to close the
   square. No scene objects for them.
6. Pedestals, bell, blocks and the player are built exactly as now, from
   the layout constants. Shadows as now.
