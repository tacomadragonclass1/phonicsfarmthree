## The three failures, with the reviewer's notes

1. **"accelerates to walkSpeed and covers roughly 12 m in 2 s" -- covers 10.1 m.**
   Likely cause: `move()` overwrites `this.velocity` with the movement the
   controller achieved and then accelerates FROM that value next step. Any
   small per-step loss (ground snap, the 0.02 offset, the downward stick
   speed sliding along the floor) is fed back and compounds, so he never
   holds walkSpeed. Keep two things apart: a private *intent* velocity that
   the acceleration integrates, and the public `velocity` that reports what
   was achieved. When an axis is clearly blocked (achieved speed below half
   of intent on that axis), pull the intent on that axis down to the
   achieved value so he does not keep pushing into a wall.
   Expected after the fix: steady speed within 2% of 6.4 m/s on flat ground.

2. **"steps over a 0.15 m lip but not a 0.6 m one" -- ends at y = -0.82.**
   A negative y means he left the ground entirely. Check the test's own
   geometry first (is the ground wide enough for the distance walked, and is
   the lip resting ON the ground rather than replacing it?) before changing
   the controller. Then confirm autostep really is active for static
   colliders in this Rapier version.

3. **"isBoxClear is false against a block" -- returns true.**
   The overlap query does not see the block. Check, in this order: that the
   block collider in the test really overlaps the queried box; that the
   `filterGroups` argument has membership and mask the right way round for a
   QUERY (compare with how `canReach`, which passes, builds its groups);
   that the argument order of `intersectionsWithShape` matches
   `node_modules/@dimforge/rapier3d-compat/dist/pipeline/world.d.ts`.
